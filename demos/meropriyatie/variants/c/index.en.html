About the forumA city starts
at your front door

Block is a one-day forum about what you can see from your window: a courtyard where everyone parks on the grass, a bus stop with no shelter, a sidewalk that shrinks to a narrow path every winter. We skip the master plans and glossy renderings. We care about fixes you can ship in a single season and check with numbers.

Our speakers are traffic engineers, landscape architects, tenant-association chairs and researchers: people who have already changed something. Each talk runs 25 minutes, with before-and-after data and an honest look at what went wrong, followed by questions from the room. Attendees get slides and recordings within a week.

Courtyards

Parking, play areas, lighting, snow, and how to get the neighbors on board.

Transit

Bus stops, dedicated lanes, bike networks and sidewalk widths.

Public space

Ground floors, pocket parks, plazas, and how to tell whether they work.
