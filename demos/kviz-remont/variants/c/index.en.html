    01 — how we work

    Four stages and a sign-off after each

    Pay by stage. Every Friday you get photos and a short report by text, so you never have to drive to the site.

    1. 1–2 days
      Stage 01

      Measure and quote

      We measure with lasers and check the walls and utilities. The itemized quote arrives the next day.

    2. 2–4 weeks
      Stage 02

      Rough-in work

      Demolition, wiring, pipes, plaster and leveling screed: everything that disappears behind the finishes.

    3. 2–5 weeks
      Stage 03

      Finish work

      Tile, floors, paint or wallpaper, ceilings, doors, outlets and plumbing fixtures.

    4. 1 day
      Stage 04

      Handover

      We walk the punch list together, haul away the debris, and hand over the keys, sign-off and warranty card.
